01 / Featured project

MamaCare Kenya

MamaCare Kenya is a mobile-first maternal and newborn support platform designed to help new mothers feel informed, organized, and supported during the early postpartum and newborn period.

The project brings together practical newborn education, stage-based guidance, baby-care tracking, warning signs, medication information, and care-finding tools in one simple experience.
